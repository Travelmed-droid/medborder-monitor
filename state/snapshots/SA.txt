Hajj and Umrah
The Saudi Food and Drug Authority (SFDA) participates, along with several government sectors, in serving pilgrims during the Hajj season. This is in compliance with the directives of the wise leadership—may God preserve its glory—whose directives always focus on providing the best and most advanced services to pilgrims visiting the Two Holy Mosques, harnessing all human and technical capabilities and energies to serve them.

SFDA Tamani app
The Tamani app provides consumers with accurate information about products regulated by the Saudi Food and Drug Authority (SFDA), aiming to improve their health and nutritional habits. When searching by product name or scanning its barcode, the app displays all relevant information.
