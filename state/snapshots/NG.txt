Blacklist of Companies
The Blacklist of Companies page provides an official and regularly updated record of companies that have been identified by NAFDAC as non‑compliant with regulatory standards. It highlights organizations that have engaged in serious or repeated violations, including poor manufacturing practices, submission of falsified documents, quality defects, and breaches of importation or licensing requirements.

Watchlist of Companies
The Watchlist of Companies page provides an official and regularly updated record of companies that have been identified by NAFDAC as non‑compliant with regulatory standards. It highlights organizations that have engaged in serious or repeated violations, including poor manufacturing practices, submission of falsified documents, quality defects, and breaches of importation or licensing requirements.
