Limited availability of Azactam 1 g and 2 g powder for solution for injection/infusion: recommendations for specialist doctors and hospital pharmacists
08/07/2026
Due to supply issues, Azactam 1 g and Azactam 2 g powder for solution for injection/infusion are currently only available in very limited quantities on the Belgian market. The available stock is not sufficient to meet patients’ needs during the expected period of shortage. Production is not expected to resume before July 2027. The FAMHP's task force on Unavailability issues recommendations.
